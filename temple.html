A Living Temple

Jayanti Devi Mandir is more than a monument to an ancient tradition.

It remains an active place of worship, with daily prayers and regular religious observances. Local reporting identifies Pandit Naveen Shastri as the temple priest and describes daily worship of Maa Jayanti Devi.

The temple hosts religious observances including yajnas, aarti, bhajan programmes, bhandaras and special celebrations during Navratri and other festivals.
